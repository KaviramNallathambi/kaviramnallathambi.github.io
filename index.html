🤔 What Is chat-seek-vscode?

chat-seek-vscode is a powerful local search tool that lives right inside your Visual Studio Code editor. It helps you find any past conversation you've had with AI assistants like Claude Code, Codex, or OpenCode—all without leaving your workspace.

Think of it as a search engine for your chats. Instead of scrolling endlessly through old conversation logs, you simply type what you remember, and chat-seek-vscode finds the exact message or conversation instantly. It's fast, private (everything stays on your computer), and incredibly easy to use.

✨ What makes it special?

It uses a smart technology called "Laya reranking" which understands the meaning behind your search words. So even if you don't remember the exact phrase, you'll still find the right conversation. It's like having a personal assistant who remembers everything for you!
